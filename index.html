Started with two buffaloes and my father's cycle.

In 2007 my father Gopal-ji used to carry two cans on a cycle through these same lanes. My uncle taught me how to check milk by hand and nose before it ever reaches a customer. I still do it every morning at 4:30.

Today we have a small shed, a steel boiler and a van, but the rule has not changed. If I would not give it to my own mother, it does not go out.

Come by the shop any evening. Taste the curd and see the kitchen yourself.
